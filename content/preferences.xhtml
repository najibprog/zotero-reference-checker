With a personal Elsevier API key, “Add” and “Find PDF” can download ScienceDirect PDFs through Elsevier's official API — for articles your institution gives you access to (on campus or VPN). Your key stays in your Zotero preferences and is only sent to api.elsevier.com. Get a free key at https://dev.elsevier.com/apikey/manage The institutional token is only needed to download off campus; ask your library or Elsevier support for it.
